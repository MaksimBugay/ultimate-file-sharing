Opening the joint link connects the receiver and requests camera and microphone permission. The caller chooses end-to-end encryption before sharing the link; the setting stays fixed during the call. With encryption enabled, the link contains the caller's public key. Each browser keeps its private key on that device and derives a shared media secret after the receiver joins. Compare the security codes through another trusted channel before both people confirm the call. The optional extra echo setting is also included in the link. It uses playback positions sent with audio chunks to find matching audio in your local recording, then suppresses that echo in incoming playback. Recording starts automatically after both people confirm the security code. Camera off sends black frames, and microphone mute sends silence. Incoming chunks play through one MediaSource with separate audio and video buffers. After the call, save one local recording with both audio and video. Set the chunk interval with ?chunkSeconds=0.5; the default is 1 second. Recording needs HTTPS or localhost.
