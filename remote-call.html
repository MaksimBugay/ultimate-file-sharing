Audio and video buffer diagnostics appear during replay.

Turn Camera on off at any time to stop camera capture while the continuous video recorder produces black frames. Turning it back on resumes camera frames without restarting either recorder. Delayed replay starts automatically while recording and uses one MediaSource with separate audio and video SourceBuffers. Save audio from 12–5s ago creates a separate .wav microphone clip. Saving after recording creates one video-only .webm file and one audio-only .webm file. Chunk start and end times are milliseconds on a common monotonic clock. Set the chunk interval with ?chunkSeconds=0.5; the default is 1 second. Recording needs HTTPS or localhost.
